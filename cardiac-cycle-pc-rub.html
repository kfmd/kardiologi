Clinical key: Only 3 of 7 phases produce enough cardiac displacement for a pericardial friction rub: triphasic (Phases 1+3+6). Loss of Phase 1 (e.g. AF) → biphasic. Only Phase 3 → monophasic. The systolic component (Phase 3) is always the loudest.
