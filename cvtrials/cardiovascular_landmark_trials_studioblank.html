Landmark Cardiovascular Trials & Studies

A chronological, clinically oriented index of major studies that shaped contemporary ESC and AHA/ACC cardiovascular practice. The collection begins with the Framingham Heart Study (1948) and follows the evidence pathway into modern prevention, hypertension, lipids, diabetes, coronary disease, ACS, heart failure, AF, devices, valves, HCM, PAD, pulmonary hypertension, infective endocarditis, pericarditis and perioperative cardiovascular medicine.

How to read this atlas

Framingham and several other entries are observational cohorts or epidemiologic studies, not randomized clinical trials. The table intentionally includes both positive and negative randomized studies because guidelines are shaped by evidence showing what works, what does not, and which patient phenotypes modify the treatment effect.

LANDMARK EVIDENCE ≠ EVERY EVIDENCE

This is a curated educational index rather than a systematic review of every cardiovascular trial. Study descriptions are intentionally concise. Before using an individual result to guide patient care, consult the primary publication and the applicable current ESC/AHA/ACC guideline.
